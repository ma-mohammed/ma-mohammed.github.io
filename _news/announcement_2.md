Our paper ["Maps are good but are simpler maps better? Insights on urban bicycling in the US"](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ae3945) is now published in _Environmental Research Communications_ (8(2), 025004). We ran a national survey of cyclists to understand whether simplified schematic maps actually change how people navigate and choose routes — turns out the answer is more nuanced than expected. Worth a read if you work on cycling infrastructure or behavior.
