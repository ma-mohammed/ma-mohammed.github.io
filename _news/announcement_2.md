["Maps are good but are simpler maps better? Insights on urban bicycling in the US"](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ae3945) published in _Environmental Research Communications_, 8(2), 025004. This paper examines whether schematic bicycle maps influence cycling behavior and mode choice, drawing on a national survey of US cyclists.
