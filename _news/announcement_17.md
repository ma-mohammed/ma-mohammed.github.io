Our paper ["Can metro-style schematic maps induce micromobility ridership? A Bayesian latent class ordered logistic analysis"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-026-00172-6) is published in _Data Science for Transportation_ (Vol. 8, Article 30). The paper uses a Bayesian latent class ordered logit to model stated intentions to ride under different map designs, separating riders into groups with different design sensitivities. This is the second paper with Andrew Ruger, an REU student I mentored in Summer 2025.
