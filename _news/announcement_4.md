Our paper "Maps are good but are simpler maps better? Insights on urban bicycling in the US" has been published in _Environmental Research Communications_.

---

This work examines whether schematic (simplified) bicycle maps are more effective than standard maps at influencing cycling behavior and mode choice. Using survey data from cyclists across the US, we analyze preferences for different map design features and their relationship to cycling intentions.

The paper is now available in _Environmental Research Communications_ (Volume 8, Issue 2, 025004). [Read it here.](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ae3945)
