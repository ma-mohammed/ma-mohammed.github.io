["Extracting Spatiotemporal Bus Passenger Trip Typologies from Noisy Mobile Ticketing Boarding Data"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-023-00082-x) is published in _Data Science for Transportation_ (Vol. 5, Article 20). This is the first paper in the PVTA transit series — using hierarchical clustering and DTW to find passenger behavioral groups from raw mobile ticketing activations, which are messy but contain a lot of signal if you look in the right way.
