["Extracting Spatiotemporal Bus Passenger Trip Typologies from Noisy Mobile Ticketing Boarding Data"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-023-00082-x) published in _Data Science for Transportation_, 5, 20 (2023). This paper develops a clustering framework using hierarchical methods and dynamic time warping to identify passenger behavioral typologies in the Pioneer Valley bus system.
