["A Roadway Crash Typology of Census Tracts Enables Targeted Interventions via Interpretable Machine Learning"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-025-00128-2) published in _Data Science for Transportation_, 7, 14 (2025). This work classifies 2,480 New England census tracts into crash typologies using UMAP and Gaussian mixture models, then forecasts crash risk using gradient boosting with SHAP interpretation.
