["Enhanced Seasonal Typology-Informed Transit Trip Chaining via Mobile Boarding and Survey Data"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-024-00108-y) published in _Data Science for Transportation_, 6, 19 (2024). This paper integrates passenger typology and seasonal patterns into a trip chaining model, with gradient boosting spatial error correction reducing MAE by 70%.
