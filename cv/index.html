Research Experience

  • Jan 2021
    – Present
    Graduate Research Assistant
    University of Massachusetts Amherst, Amherst, MA
    • Schematic Bicycle Map Design and Micromobility Behavior
      • Mapped the national landscape of schematic bicycle map adoption across 684 US urban counties; Bayesian mixture modeling with XGBoost and SHAP analysis revealed three county typologies of adoption.
      • Designed and fielded an incentivized national survey on how cyclists and micromobility riders respond to map design: simplification level, geographic accuracy, infrastructure representation, and route legibility.
      • Modeled stated intentions to ride under different map designs with a Bayesian latent class ordered logit that separates riders into groups with different design sensitivities.
      • Formulated a mixed-integer linear program that selects bicycle map routes from street and trail networks under safety, connectivity, and user-preference constraints.
      • Built a bot detection pipeline for online surveys that combines platform signals, browser fingerprinting, timing checks, and an XGBoost validation model.
      • Funding: Armstrong Fund for Science, 2024-2026 (PI: J. Oke); NSF REU, 2023-2025.
      • Publications: 2 published, 3 under review, 1 in preparation.
    • Spatial Crash Typology and Risk Prediction
      • Classified 2,480 New England census tracts into crash typologies with UMAP and Gaussian mixture models, using one year of state-level crash records.
      • Linked patterns in crash frequency and severity to roadway geometry, land use, and socioeconomic conditions across urban, suburban, and rural tracts.
      • Trained XGBoost models with SHAP interpretation to predict crash risk within each typology and show which factors drive risk where, informing where safety interventions should go.
      • Funding: New England University Transportation Center (NEUTC), 2024-2025 (PI: J. Oke).
      • Publications: 1 published.
    • Transit OD Inference from Partial Mobile Ticketing Data
      • Built a framework that infers full origin-destination-transfer matrices from boarding-only mobile ticketing data on a regional bus network with 40+ routes.
      • Clustered 40,000+ weekly riders into passenger typologies using hierarchical clustering with dynamic time warping.
      • Added a gradient boosting spatial error correction step that cut MAE by 70% and SMAPE by 85% relative to baseline trip chaining.
      • Funding: Pioneer Valley Transit Authority (PVTA), 2021-2023 (PI: J. Oke).
      • Publications: 3 published, 1 working paper.
